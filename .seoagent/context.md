- **Type:** product (e-commerce + a free utility tool, not SaaS)
- **Audience:** US/Canada drivers shopping for a personalized ("vanity") license plate, plus gift buyers looking for a decorative car accessory (Hello Kitty, Snoopy, Veteran, etc. themed frames suggest a broad consumer-gifting audience, skewing toward car enthusiasts and people buying for someone else)
- **Industry:** Automotive accessories / DMV utility tool
- **Location:** Unknown — do not assume or state a specific business location/jurisdiction anywhere in generated content
- **Description:** Two things under one brand: (1) a personalized license plate availability finder that checks real-time availability against official state DMV systems (currently live for WV, KS, ND, TN, ME, OH, KY, WY — more states added over time; see lib/plates/adapters/registry.ts for the current list, don't hardcode a stale list into content), and (2) handmade, epoxy-finished, 3D-printed license plate frames sold via Stripe checkout and Etsy (decoforge3d.etsy.com).

# Writing Instructions

- Plate availability results are never guaranteed — always say "appears available" / "check the official DMV" language, never promise a plate is definitely free. This matches the site's own Terms of Service.
- Don't invent or assume which states have live checking — verify against the current site/registry rather than listing states from memory in an article, since the list grows over time.
- Keep tone light and approachable, not overly corporate — the product line itself is playful (novelty/gift frames).
